# Introduction

Effective communication is essential for the successful execution of any project. The Communication Plan defines how team members share information, coordinate activities, discuss project progress, resolve issues, and ensure smooth collaboration throughout the development lifecycle of OptiCrop.

A structured communication strategy helps improve teamwork, transparency, accountability, and project efficiency .

---

# 🎯 Objectives of Communication

The communication plan aims to:

* Ensure clear information sharing among team members.
* Facilitate coordination of project activities.
* Track project progress and milestones.
* Resolve issues and challenges quickly.
* Support collaborative decision-making.
* Maintain proper project documentation.

# ⚠️ Issue Escalation Process

If issues arise during project development:

### Step 1

Identify and document the issue.

### Step 2

Discuss within the team.

### Step 3

Seek guidance from the Team Lead.

### Step 4

Consult the Mentor if required.

### Step 5

Implement the agreed solution.
