# 🗺️ Customer Journey Map

## Project Title

🌾 **OptiCrop – Smart Agricultural Production Optimization Engine**

---

## Introduction

A Customer Journey Map illustrates the complete experience of a user while interacting with the OptiCrop system. It helps identify user goals, actions, expectations, and system touchpoints throughout the crop recommendation process.

For OptiCrop, the primary customer is a farmer seeking data-driven crop recommendations based on soil and environmental conditions.

---

# 👨‍🌾 Customer Persona

## Primary User: Farmer

### Profile

* Engaged in agricultural activities
* Needs support for crop selection
* Wants to improve productivity and profitability
* May have limited technical knowledge

---

# 🎯 Customer Goal

The farmer wants to identify the most suitable crop for cultivation based on current soil and environmental conditions.

---

# 📋 Customer Journey Stages

| Stage           | Customer Action                | Thoughts & Expectations               | OptiCrop Support                               |
| --------------- | ------------------------------ | ------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------- |
| Awareness       | Learns about OptiCrop          | Looking for better farming solutions  | Introduces AI-powered crop recommendations     |
| Exploration     | Visits the platform            | Wants an easy-to-use system           | Simple and intuitive interface                 |
| Data Entry      | Enters agricultural parameters | Expects accurate analysis             | Input validation and guidance                  |
| Prediction      | Requests crop recommendation   | Wants fast and reliable results       | ML-based prediction engine                     |
| Results         | Reviews recommendations        | Looks for confidence and alternatives | Displays recommended crop and confidence score |
| Decision Making | Chooses crop for cultivation   | Wants to reduce farming risks         | Data-driven recommendations                    |
| Outcome         | Implements recommendation      | Expects improved productivity         | Supports better agricultural decisions         |
