\# 📋 Business Requirements



\## Functional Requirements



\### FR1: Crop Recommendation

\- System shall accept 7 soil and environmental parameters

\- System shall recommend the most suitable crop

\- System shall display confidence percentage



\### FR2: Top 3 Predictions

\- System shall show top 3 crop matches

\- Each match shall have a confidence score



\### FR3: User Interface

\- System shall have a responsive web interface

\- Navigation: Home, Features, Recommendation, About, Contact



\### FR4: Data Management

\- System shall use CSV dataset with 22 crops

\- System shall handle missing values



\## Non-Functional Requirements



\### NFR1: Performance

\- Predictions shall take less than 2 seconds



\### NFR2: Usability

\- Interface shall be intuitive for non-technical users



\### NFR3: Reliability

\- Model accuracy shall be above 95%
